About FertilityBridge Africa

Common questions

What you may want to know

These general answers are a starting point. Your chosen clinic should confirm the details for your circumstances.

Do I need to travel to Kenya for the first consultation?

Not necessarily. An introductory discussion may be possible online. The fertility provider will advise whether an in-person assessment is required and when travel may be appropriate.

Can I complete some tests in my current country?

Possibly. Ask the treating clinic which tests can be completed locally, how recent they must be and what laboratory or imaging standards they require.

How much does fertility treatment cost?

Costs vary by clinic, treatment plan, medication, tests and individual needs. Request a written, itemised estimate directly from the provider before making travel or payment decisions.

Do you provide medical advice?

No. FertilityBridge Africa provides coordination and general information. Diagnosis, treatment recommendations, prescriptions and clinical care must come from appropriately licensed healthcare professionals.

Can you help with accommodation and local arrangements?

We can discuss practical coordination options such as appointment scheduling and accommodation enquiries. Confirm all arrangements, costs and cancellation terms before booking.
